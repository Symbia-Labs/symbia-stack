## What shipped

**Receipts at the emit boundary.** A port declares evidence alongside its lane:
`recipe` (the operation and its resolved inputs), `witness` (a digest of the
bytes as received), or `none` with a note. Omission is the strict path — a port
declared canonical requires a recipe unless it says otherwise, and one that
emits without evidence is downgraded to apocryphal carrying `laneReason`.

`normaliseEmission` took a boolean, so the per-port `lanes` block published in
every manifest reached no code. Sixteen of sixteen components already declared
a lane. Coverage was never the gap.

**The session envelope.** `imagine.session.opened` is written at ledger
creation, carrying `t0`, the session fingerprint, its own lane, and what it does
not assert. The envelope had declared a stop and no start across all 36 ledgers
checked. `session-time.mjs` derives placement between the anchors under a named
residual policy that travels in the receipt.

**The attachment.** The host mints 32 random bytes at spawn into a 0600 address
file and requires them on every route. Eight routes across four services had
answered an unauthenticated local caller. Nothing to paste, nothing to rotate.

**One stack, two packagings.** `experiments/standalone` → `imagine/`.
`npm run package:imagine` builds a 688 KB plugin from service source and
refuses to ship a tree containing a key or a path an installer rejects. The
persistent packaging is unchanged and now verified: 13 containers healthy, 390
operations through the same MCP server.

See `docs/PACKAGING.md`.

## The pattern in the day's failures

- A stack booted 10 of 10 with an empty catalog and an assistant loader
  retrying against nothing.
- `ready: true` with two services mounted and eight failed, because ready meant
  "boot finished" rather than "boot finished and nothing failed". Making it

None of these threw. Each was found by a number being wrong.

## Session close

Sealed: `imagine/.session/bundle-1786930058927.json`, 172 trace entries, **0
artifacts**. Zero is correct and worth stating: every `reload.sh` starts a new
session with a fresh store, and the day's 28 MAP writes are spread across 55
ledgers belonging to hosts that no longer exist. 56,645 events recorded, 18
bundles sealed, and the one sealed at close holds almost none of it.

A per-session key is the right construction for an ephemeral stack. It also
means a day's work does not accumulate into one record, and nothing today
carried a session across a reload. That is a gap in the dogfooding, not in the
mechanism.
